# What we stand for

Your potential, all mediums.

Anyone, with anything they own, can create for any medium at the highest quality. What they create stays theirs.

## Values

::: cols
## Anyone

**Inclusion and attribution.** Everyone means everyone, and the maker's name travels with the work.

## Easy

**Quality made simple.** The rules live in the tool, so the best possible file is the default.

## Honest

**Attribution and consent.** The file says who made it, and nothing leaves your device unless you asked.
:::

## Mission

Creative trust amongst the infinite.

Generated content is about to be infinite. When anything can be made, what stays scarce is trust: who made this, from what, and whether it is what it claims. Lolly is the press, not the sketch. The committed step, reproducible, on your own device.

## Mantra

> What stays so everything else can change.

For a person, that is your name, your decision and your work. For a brand, it is the identity that holds while every trend moves around it.
